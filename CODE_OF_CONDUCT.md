# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility and apologize when mistakes happen.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing sensitive logs or transcripts.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic conflict.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
